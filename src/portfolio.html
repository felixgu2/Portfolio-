I'm Tun. I studied Computer Science with a major in Cybersecurity at the University of Wollongong, after a Diploma in Information Technology at SIM Global Education.

Security interests me because it asks you to be precise. A system is only as safe as its weakest assumption, so you learn to question every one. Cryptography is where that precision matters most: in SecureVault, a single changed byte is enough for authenticated encryption to refuse the file.

I like building things end to end, from encryption logic in Rust to a full-stack app shipped in two days. I'm now back home in Yangon and looking for my first role, where I can keep learning from people who care about doing things properly.
